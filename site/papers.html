How Papers Are Scored (0–10)

Paper Score

Overall quality: citation impact, recency, venue rank (A* > A > arXiv), acceptance tier (oral/spotlight), topic relevance, and completeness of metadata.

Read First Score

How worth reading this paper is right now: novelty signal, trending topic alignment, and how recently it was published.

Content Potential

How well-suited the paper is for content creation: clarity of contribution, broad appeal, and available abstract quality.

Scores are computed automatically from metadata — not expert peer review. Conference papers (ICLR, NeurIPS, etc.) receive a venue bonus, and oral/spotlight acceptances receive an extra boost.
