IV. Method · Collect, score, store, refresh 004 / 011
Method

How ResearchScope actually works.

We index 60,000+ papers from arXiv CoRR and 20+ major conference proceedings — all served from a Railway PostgreSQL backend so you can browse and search the full dataset with no caps.

I.
Collect

Pull every new CS paper from arXiv CoRR (19 categories) plus proceedings from NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, ACL, KDD, AAAI and 15+ more venues.

II.
Score →

Each paper gets a Paper Score 0–10 across 6 signals: recency, novelty, citation quality, institution prestige, author reputation, and completeness.

III.
Store →

60,000+ papers land in Railway PostgreSQL — fully browsable and searchable with server-side filtering and pagination, no caps.

IV.
Refresh →

arXiv papers update daily (Mon–Fri). Conference papers are permanent — added once per proceedings cycle, never expire.

▾ The scoring system, in plain English
I.Recency · 28%

A paper published this year scores 10/10. One from 10+ years ago scores 0. CS moves fast — freshness matters.

II.Novelty · 22%

We scan the title and abstract for words like "we propose", "outperforms", "state-of-the-art". Surveys and replications score lower.

III.Citation Quality · 18%

Log-scaled citation count plus conference rank (A* = top-tier), with an extra boost for oral/spotlight acceptances. A NeurIPS oral with 500 citations scores near 10.

IV.Institution Prestige · 15%

Papers from OpenAI, DeepMind, Google Research, MIT, Stanford, CMU etc. get a boost. Detected from affiliations or abstract text.

V.Author Reputation · 10%

Papers co-authored by Turing Award winners, lab founders, or well-known researchers (Hinton, LeCun, Vaswani, Karpathy…) score higher.

VI.Completeness · 7%

Has the paper got an abstract, author list, PDF link, and topic tags? Well-documented papers surface above poorly indexed ones.

Author momentum — who is on a hot streak?

Author momentum measures how productive and impactful a researcher has been recently — not just their all-time record. It combines recent output (30%), average paper quality (25%), acceleration vs. their prior two-year window (20%), topic strength in trending areas (15%), and conference strength (10%). A senior professor with 10,000 citations but no papers in 3 years scores low. A PhD student publishing 4 strong LLM papers this year at NeurIPS scores high. Momentum finds who is actively pushing the frontier right now.

XI. Changelog · What changed 011 / 011
Changelog

What’s new in ResearchScope.

Actively maintained
Jul 2026

Three-theme interface — Atelier Zero, Industrial Brutalist & Field Notes

Choose your reading environment from the nav — the warm editorial Atelier Zero, a high-contrast Industrial Brutalist, or the tactile Field Notes. Your pick persists across every page and reload with no flash of the wrong theme. Ships with a rebuilt responsive navigation (grouped menus, search, auth controls, mobile) and a modular CSS architecture for shared foundations, components, motion, and contrast.

Jun 2026

OpenReview acceptance tiers — Oral & Spotlight signals

Papers from ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, and COLM now carry their acceptance tier (oral / spotlight / poster). Oral and spotlight papers get a score boost and show as badges. Coverage extended through ICLR 2026, NeurIPS 2025, and ICML 2025.

Jun 2026

Smarter Conference & Journal recommenders

Both recommenders now run on one shared matching engine. New: one-click sample abstracts, field auto-detection with a confidence level, a fit-scale legend, ⌘/Ctrl+Enter to run, auto-saved drafts, and a Copy-shortlist export.

Jun 2026

Keyless journal fetching via OpenAlex

The pipeline can now pull every paper from 20 top CS journals (TPAMI, IJCV, AIJ, PR, TKDE, Nature Communications…) directly through OpenAlex by source ID — no Semantic Scholar API key required. S2 is still used automatically when a key is configured.

Jun 2026

Visual refresh — Atelier Zero skin

Site-wide rebrand: warm paper canvas, Inter Tight + Playfair Display typography, single coral accent, italic-serif Roman numerals, side rails and metadata strip, hairline borders. Now the default of the three-theme interface.

Jun 2026

Dedicated Sign-in & Register pages

Sign-in and Create Account are now separate focused pages instead of a shared tabbed modal. Each page redirects back to where you came from after auth.

Jun 2026

Security hardening — Auth & API

JWT secret misconfiguration now raises a loud server warning; API errors return a generic message instead of leaking internal details; SSL mode is conditional for local dev; login no longer saves partial auth state on profile-fetch failure.

Jun 2026

Journal recommender + 20 Q1 journals

New Journal Recommender matches your manuscript against Q1 journal profiles — impact factor, review timeline, open access status, and similar published papers. Covers JMLR, TMLR, TACL, TPAMI, Nature MI, CSUR, and 14 more.

Jun 2026

Backend API on Railway + user accounts

FastAPI backend deployed on Railway with PostgreSQL full-text search. Sign up to save favourites and sync across devices. API available at railway.app/docs.

Jun 2026

OpenAlex integration + HF training dataset

Papers now sourced from OpenAlex (250M+ works) in addition to arXiv, S2, ACL, OpenReview, PMLR, and CVF. Full dataset pushed to HuggingFace as JSONL for LLM training.

Jun 2026

Complete A* conference coverage

AAAI, IJCAI, CHI, SIGIR, WWW, KDD, WSDM, SIGMOD, ICSE now bulk-fetched via Semantic Scholar alongside ICLR, NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, ACL, EMNLP and more.

Apr 2026

CiteLens integration — citation analysis in one click

Every arXiv paper and Paper of the Day has an "Analyze citations" button linking to CiteLens — see who cited it, ranked by impact and relevance.
